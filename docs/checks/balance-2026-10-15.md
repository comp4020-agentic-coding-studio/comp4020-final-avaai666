# Balance, 2026-10-15 (bots only)

Written by `node scripts/balance.ts`. Every match here is bots against bots, with the strategies in scripts/balance.ts, on the seeds `balance-<mode>-1` to `-300`. These are claims about these bots, strategies and seeds, not about how people play. A fair share in a four-seat match is 25%; the target is that no strategy wins more than 40%. 95% intervals are Wilson intervals.

## Changes

- Step 3, Who's Poaching. Rules (DESIGN v3.1): a last meeting after day 3
  with no Skip; a meeting with no exile costs the lake 5 health. Number:
  `POACH.FINE_CD` 0.6 → 1.5 s. Why: with 0.6 s, the bot poacher's fine net
  took the lake below 40% on day 1 in 39 of 40 seeded games, so no vote
  could matter (fishers won 0–4% whatever the table did). Tried 1.5, 1.6,
  1.8, 2.4 and 3.0; 1.5 is the value where "vote on buoy evidence" is inside
  45–70% (67.7%) and "always skip" still loses (0%). Before: always skip 0.0%,
  accused most 1.0%, buoy evidence 4.0%.
- Step 4, Boat Rush. Rule (session 14): the first upgrade costs 3 banked
  fish and the second 7 (was 6–8 each). Numbers: `RUSH.UPGRADES.fastHull.speed`
  1.2 → 1.12, `wideNet.net` 1.25 → 1.12, and `RUSH.LOAD_SLOW` 0.35 → 0.6 (a
  full hold is 60% slower; /b keeps boat-sim's 0.35). Big Hold stays +5,
  because DESIGN.md names that number and a test checks it; Fast Hull stays
  at 1.12 or more, because a session-13 test requires it to be more than 10%
  faster. Why: with cheaper upgrades, "Big Hold first" won 52% and "Wide Net
  first" 41.5%; single-upgrade trials (120 seeds) showed Big Hold alone is
  worth nothing and Fast Hull alone is the strongest, so the edge was the
  pair. A slower full hold costs most to the boats that carry most. Also
  in the harness: lineups were a fixed rotation, which gave each strategy
  the same rivals every time; they are now a seeded shuffle.
  Result: the three upgrade-first paths win 39.6–40.9%; the worst is Fast
  Hull first at 40.9% (95% interval 34.4–47.7%), which is on the target, not
  under it. "No upgrades" (5.6%) and "ram-heavy" (0.0%) are weak; ram-heavy
  is weak because this script chases boats instead of fishing.
  Accepted by Ava (session 14): Rush "Fast Hull first" at 40.9% (34.4–47.7%)
  is accepted. Its interval covers 40% and two strategies seldom win.
- Step 5, Lake Holdings. Rules (session 14): a net takes 2 from every school
  on or beside it (was up to 3 in total); a school below `HOLD.DRY_FISH` (3)
  dies out and its tile runs dry until a pond beside it brings it back; a
  school sitting on dry water loses a fish a tide. Number: `HOLD.POND_BREED`
  2 → 3, so a pond's new school is not below the dry size the moment it is
  born. No strategy was over 40% before or after.
- Step 6, Fishing Party. Rules (session 14): the last-placed player's
  variant pick is its own 4 s screen before the shop, and only that player
  is sent the choice; bait pulls only fish within two lantern radii, for the
  first 10 s of the round. Number: `PARTY.FAST` (Fast Boat) 1.35 → 1.15.
  Why: "Fast Boat always" won 53.3% before these changes and 60.7% after
  (bait weakened, so the speed edge grew). At 1.15 no strategy is over 40%
  and Fast Boat still pays (34.7%); at 1.08 "never buy" rose to 39.4%.
