A New Year print, and a warning

年年有余

“Fish to spare, every year.” The blessing on every New Year print: 余 (to spare) sounds like 鱼 (fish).

竭泽而渔，岂不获得？而明年无鱼。

“Drain the lake and of course you catch the fish. But next year there are no fish.” Lüshi Chunqiu, c. 239 BC

撒网Tap the water to throw your net. Every fish inside the ring is yours.
春In spring, two fish that meet lay eggs. The fry grow up over winter.
冬In winter the lake freezes and the village meets to choose a rule.

Four families share the lake. The other three here are Old Wang, Jin and Mei; in the real game they are the people at your table. Five years, about four minutes.

Winter · the village meets

Which rule does the village keep next year?

A rule wins with more votes than any other and at least half the village. Nobody is stopped from breaking it; everyone sees who does.
