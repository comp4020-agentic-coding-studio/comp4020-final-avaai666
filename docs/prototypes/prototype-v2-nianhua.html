A New Year print, and a warning

年年有余

“Fish to spare, every year.” The blessing on every New Year print: 余 (to spare) sounds like 鱼 (fish).

竭泽而渔，岂不获得？而明年无鱼。

“Drain the lake to catch the fish, and of course you catch them. But next year there are no fish.”

Lüshi Chunqiu, c. 239 BC

Four families share one lake. Tap the water to cast your net. Every fish you land is yours. You can buy a fine-mesh net that lands more than twice as many. Six years. Then we see what you did to the lake.

In the real game the other families are the people at your table. Here, Old Wang, Jin and Mei play them.
