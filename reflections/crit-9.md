# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

Playing my own prototype. The model was finished, tested and green, and the
game was not fun. Where you threw did not matter, and the other players could
only be watched. No test could have told me that, because every test checked
what I had asked for. I stopped and rebuilt the fish as animals that school,
meet and spawn, so the thing you aim at is the thing that runs out.

The second breakthrough came from someone else reading my numbers. A review
showed my simulation flattered my own claim. Fixed, the claim got smaller:
a fine net pays the first three families, not the fourth. The smaller claim
is the true one, and it is a better lesson.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I used to protect a claim once it had a test. Now I think a test can guard a
mistake as carefully as a truth. The agent's tests only showed that the code
matched my model; nobody had checked the model.

I want to be the developer who shows her work to a reader who wants to find
the hole, and who changes the claim when they find one, instead of tuning the
numbers until the old claim fits.
